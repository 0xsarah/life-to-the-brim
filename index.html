Full Speech · Life to the Brim

Opening

I once saw a picture in a magazine that I could never forget. It showed two kinds of people. One was a craftsman—someone who spends 50 years making the same wooden chair. The other was an explorer—someone who learns one thing, masters it, and then moves on to a new adventure. I realized I didn't want to do just one thing. I wanted to live many lives in one lifetime. I wanted my life to be at the brim.

Life 1: The Golden Script

My first life was in traditional banking at Bank of America. It was stable and prestigious. But I wanted more. So I moved to Ant Financial. That was a whirlwind. I saw first-hand how China became a cashless society. It was exciting to be at the center of a fintech revolution. But this life came with a price. I lost my balance. My weekends disappeared. My phone never stopped ringing with work calls. Those long, relaxing vacations became a distant memory. I was successful, but I was exhausted. I had mastered the world of fintech, and I knew it was time for my next rebirth.

Life 2: The Digital Frontier

Four years later, I jumped into the "wild west" of Web3. This is where I am now. It was a huge pivot. In this world, the line between what is real and what is fake is very thin. But this is where I found my real power. I realized that my experience in the "old" world of banking and the "fast" world of fintech gave me a unique edge. I wasn't just starting over; I was combining my lives. I learned to see the truth behind the hype. I learned how to build digital infrastructure that can actually change a nation.

Conclusion

Today, I run my company, SIGN. We build the future for nations. But is this my final stop? No. I am no longer afraid of the unknown. To me, the future is a blank page. Fellow Toastmasters, don't just pick one path because it's safe. Be brave enough to break your old self and keep pouring new colors into your life, until your life is at the brim.
